## John Doe's Feedback

### Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)
- **Best practice:** Use Excel or PowerPoint for WBS
  - Makes it interactive and easy to update
- **Each WBS item should have:**
  - An owner (person responsible)
  - Critical items marked (needed for milestones or subsequent phases)

### Schedule
- Schedule is built on top of the WBS
- **One-to-one mapping** between WBS items and schedule items
- **Gantt charts** are good and commonly used, but other formats work too
  - Example shown: Each team member and their responsibilities listed individually
- **Include deliverables/milestones:**
  - Depict as diamonds on the schedule
  - Alternatively, show entire milestone timeline separately above the schedule

### Cost Estimates
- Create a **pie chart** showing cost breakdown:
  - Requirements
  - Design
  - Testing
  - Documentation
  - Research
  - Project management
  - etc.
- **Project management** must stay **below 10%** of total cost

### Org Charts
- **Definition:** Document listing all members of organization/project
  - Include: Pictures, names, roles
- **Should include:** John Doe and Curtin supervisor
- **Purpose:** Helps client relate to the team; pictures humanize each person

### Risk Management
- Risk must be planned for
- **Sources of risk:**
  - People getting sick
  - Team members missing from work
  - Other unforeseen circumstances
- Likely ties to the **risk register**
